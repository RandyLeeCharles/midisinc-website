MIDI Solenoid Driver (MSD)

Home | About | Progress | Gallery | Downloads | Support

The MIDI Solenoid Driver (MSD) boards are designed to allow a single MIDI channel to control groups of output drivers for pipe organs, glockenspiels, percussion, and other mechanical musical instruments.

Firmware

  • Universal Solenoid Driver Firmware

The MIDI Solenoid Driver (MSD) boards are designed using Express PCB software. The program is a free donwload and is also free to use. Find it at "expresspcb.com" use the "Classic" version.
