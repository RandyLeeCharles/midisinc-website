MIDI SINC

Building the World's First Open-Source 3D-Printed Pipe Organ

Welcome to the MIDI SINC Project.

This project combines traditional organ building, 3D printed pipes, wood pipe construction, MIDI electronics, and open-source software.

The goal is to make organ building more accessible to hobbyists, makers, experimenters, educators, woodworkers, and organ builders.
